Measured at the battery. Charged is what went into it, used is what it supplied. Bars above the line are energy in, below are energy out, and the hours under each day are how long Ferro was reading.

History

Ferro keeps every reading for 24 hours and one-minute averages for 30 days, saved on this phone. It records only while the app is open and connected, so gaps in the chart are times it wasn't.

Cell balance

One-minute averages of each cell, kept for 30 days. The bottom strip is the largest gap between cells in each minute. If balancing is working, the gap measured near the top of charge should shrink over days and weeks. At rest, compare like with like, since the gap reads larger near full and smaller in the middle.
